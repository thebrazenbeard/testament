# Yeshua Convergence V1

Status: WORKING SYNTHESIS / NOT FINAL HISTORICAL VERDICT

## High convergence at modest proposition level

### Historical person
Pauline correspondence presupposes an earlier movement centered on Yeshua/Jesus and knows named leaders connected to him.

Later Christian, hostile, Jewish and Islamic traditions diverge radically about meaning while converging on a historical center.

### Jewish/Galilean setting
Gospel traditions, names, geography, Second Temple context and material culture converge strongly at this broad level.

### Association with John
Christian traditions connect Yeshua to John; Josephus independently establishes John as a real and influential baptist figure.

The relationship is historically strong; the exact discipleship structure is not.

### Public teaching / kingdom
Multiple early Christian strata make proclamation/teaching central.

Exact sayings and interpretation remain source-dependent.

### Healing/exorcism reputation
Broad early tradition convergence supports reputation/activity at a general level.

Mechanism remains outside historical demonstration.

### Roman crucifixion under Pilate
Christian source convergence, Roman political intelligibility, Pilate's independent material/historical attestation and later external knowledge produce one of the strongest historical reconstructions.

## Medium / contested convergence

### Temple confrontation
Strong literary presence and explanatory power for execution; exact scale/meaning disputed.

### Burial
Early and plausible; exact agent/tomb details disputed.

### Resurrection experiences
Very early proclamation and named appearance traditions are strong.
What produced the experiences is unresolved.

### Women followers
Substantial early tradition support; precise roles vary.

## Low / disputed biography

### Infancy
Matthew and Luke provide distinct theological narratives with chronology/geography problems.

### Biological paternity
Canonical and later countertraditions diverge.
Panthera remains unestablished.

### Exact Passion timeline
Synoptic and Johannine calendars conflict.

### Exact self-Christology
Later texts offer increasingly explicit theological constructions; Yeshua's own self-conception remains debated.

## Principle

Convergence becomes strongest when the proposition is stated modestly.

Inflating the proposition usually lowers the evidence.

Example:
"Yeshua was crucified under Pilate" = strong.

"Every canonical trial scene reproduces the exact historical proceedings" = not strong.
