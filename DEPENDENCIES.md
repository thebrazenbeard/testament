# Dependencies

## Required upstream dependency: On-Theo

Repository:
https://github.com/thebrazenbeard/on-theo

On-Theo is the principal evidence and source-critical dependency for Testament.

Testament should consume stable IDs, exact branch/commit provenance, source records, witness records, claims, and transmission relations when available.

## Dependency contract

Permitted direction:
- On-Theo source -> Testament research note
- On-Theo claim -> Testament scene evidence
- On-Theo uncertainty -> Testament uncertainty
- On-Theo disagreement -> Testament preserved disagreement

Forbidden direction:
- Testament prose -> historical evidence
- Testament invented dialogue -> reconstructed quotation
- Testament simulation model -> ancient source meaning
- Testament theological preference -> On-Theo claim promotion

## Other research systems

Potential supporting systems may include:
- Semantic Atlas for semantic/provenance distinction;
- Rezon for structured reasoning after its contracts stabilize;
- World Zero for simulation experiments, where outputs remain model behavior rather than historical proof;
- Temporal for exact modern project events, not fabricated timestamps for ancient uncertainty.

These are optional adapters. Testament must remain readable and auditable without them.

## External source repositories and scholarly gateways

Testament may link to institutional manuscript databases, critical editions, archaeological records, and scholarly publications. Exact access date and witness/source distinction should be preserved where materially relevant.
