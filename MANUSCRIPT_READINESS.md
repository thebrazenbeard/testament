## Drafting rule

Exploratory prose may be written at any time if explicitly labeled.

Sustained canonical manuscript prose should wait until the relevant scene or sayings cluster has:
1. evidence packet;
2. contradiction/dependence check;
3. construction budget;
4. hostile review.

The exploratory Draft V1 does not waive that rule.
