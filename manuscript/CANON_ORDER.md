# Proposed Canon Order

Working title: **The Testament of the Spark**

This is an authored canon, not a claim that eight recovered ancient books exist.

A complete exploratory first prose traversal now exists under `manuscript/draft_v1/`. The order below remains the governing literary architecture; Draft V1 does not promote itself to canonical manuscript status.

1. **The World** — what kind of world could produce Yeshua.
2. **Yeshua** — the man and the recoverable life.
3. **The Sayings** — teachings disentangled from one narrative chronology.
4. **Death and What Followed** — execution, grief, visions, resurrection proclamation, movement.
5. **The Many Christs** — competing attempts to explain him.
6. **Echoes** — Jewish, Christian, mystical, Syriac, Islamic and later memories.
7. **The Kingdom Within** — explicit modern philosophical/simulation interpretation.
8. **The Testament of the Spark** — authored ethical testament on agency.

## Why this order

The reader should encounter:
world before theology;
man before doctrine;
teachings before institutional ownership;
death before resurrection system-building;
plurality before canon;
history before speculation;
speculation before the final ethical claim.
