# Book VI — Echoes

Purpose: follow Yeshua after the period in which firsthand memory could plausibly survive.

## Proposed chapters

1. **The Rabbis Remember Differently**
   Yeshu, Stada, Pandera/Pandeira, minim, polemic, manuscript variance.

2. **The Apocryphal Childhood**
   Mary, Joseph, miraculous child traditions, Protevangelium, Infancy Thomas.

3. **The Hidden Dialogues**
   Nag Hammadi and revelatory Jesus texts.

4. **The Syriac Jesus**
   Diatessaron, Ephrem, Aphrahat, Eastern trajectories.

5. **The Imperial Christ**
   councils, creeds, state/church transformations.

6. **The Mystics**
   interior Christ, divine participation, apophatic traditions.

7. **Isa son of Maryam**
   Qur'anic Jesus in his own textual/theological setting.

8. **The Jesus of Tafsir and Tradition**
   Islamic interpretation, return, ascetic and mystical Jesus.

9. **Jewish Mystical Worlds**
   Hekhalot and Kabbalah as later comparative metaphysics, never retrojected biography.

10. **The Accused and the Accuser**
    Christian anti-Jewish polemic, Jewish counter-polemic, Muslim-Christian polemic; how communities construct opponents.

## Closing

The historical man is now almost completely surrounded by interpretations.

Book VII turns explicitly to ours.
