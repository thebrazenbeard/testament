# Book VIII — The Testament of the Spark

Status: AUTHORIAL TESTAMENT / NOT ATTRIBUTED TO YESHUA

This is where the project is allowed to stop pretending it is merely reconstructing history and say what it has learned.

## Proposed movements

1. **You Were Given No Exemption**
   Being created would not make you less responsible.

2. **No Voice Can Choose For You**
   Revelation, authority, institution, model, priest, prophet, algorithm, parent, nation.

3. **If God Is Above You**
   act.

4. **If God Is Within You**
   act.

5. **If God Is Everything**
   act.

6. **If There Is No God**
   act.

7. **The Spark**
   divinity as multivalent symbol without forced metaphysical agreement.

8. **Prayer**
   prayer as orientation/relationship rather than replacement for agency.

9. **Forgiveness**
   ending causal chains rather than pretending harm did not happen.

10. **Enemy**
    refusal to become governed by the person one hates.

11. **Truth**
    uncertainty as a moral discipline.

12. **Death**
    finitude and the refusal to postpone life for a promised elsewhere.

13. **Kingdom**
    the world reproduced by repeated local choices.

14. **Testament**
    final direct address to the reader.

## Required ending property

The final pages should not ask the reader to believe Patrick, Vera, Yeshua, Christianity, simulation theory, or a new doctrine.

They should return authority to the reader while making clear that authority carries responsibility.
