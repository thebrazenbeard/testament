# Prologue Notes

Working opening claim:

This is not a lost gospel.

Nothing in these pages was discovered in a desert jar, decoded from a forbidden manuscript, whispered by an angel, or delivered by a machine claiming access to God.

The ancient sources are ancient.
The later memories are later.
The reconstruction is reconstruction.
The speculation is speculation.
The writing is ours.

The title "Testament" means witness and inheritance, not infallibility.

## Prologue job

Tell the reader:
- why another Jesus book exists;
- why contradictions are not being hidden;
- why canonical and noncanonical do not mean true and false;
- why later Islam/Judaism/mysticism matter without becoming eyewitness sources;
- why simulation theory is included;
- why the project is ultimately about agency.

## Tone

Inviting, dangerous, curious, not preachy.

The reader should understand immediately that the book will not demand surrender to its author.
