# Book V — The Many Christs

Purpose: show that "Christianity" was an argument before it was an institution.

## Proposed chapters

1. **James's Yeshua**
   Jerusalem, Torah, family, leadership.

2. **Peter's Yeshua**
   apostolic memory and later Petrine claims.

3. **Paul's Christ**
   crucified/rising Messiah, gentile inclusion, cosmic transformation.

4. **Mark's Son**
   secrecy, suffering, failure, apocalypse.

5. **Matthew's Teacher**
   Torah, fulfillment, community authority.

6. **Luke's Lord**
   Spirit, poor, outsiders, salvation history.

7. **John's Logos**
   pre-existence, signs, revelation, intimacy, cosmic identity.

8. **Thomas's Living Jesus**
   sayings, interpretation, hidden knowledge; dating/dependence kept visible.

9. **The Jewish-Christian Gospels**
   fragmentary alternatives preserved by later opponents.

10. **Marcion**
    radical discontinuity and edited canon.

11. **Valentinian Worlds**
    emanation, ignorance, revelation, inner identity.

12. **The Church That Won**
    bishops, canon pressure, rule of faith, Irenaeus, institutional memory.

## Thesis

"Many Christs" means many interpretive constructions around one historical gravitational center, not many historical Jesuses.
