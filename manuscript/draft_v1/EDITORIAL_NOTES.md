# First Draft V1 — Editorial Notes

Status: WORKING EDITORIAL FRONTIER / NOT MANUSCRIPT PROMOTION

## What this draft is

This is the first complete prose traversal of the proposed eight-book canon.

It is intentionally closer to a compressed literary-theological manuscript than a chapter outline, but it is not yet a full-length reader edition. The purpose of V1 is to test:
- whether the eight-book architecture actually reads as one work;
- whether evidence/reconstruction/authorship boundaries survive sustained prose;
- whether the ethical ending follows without requiring one metaphysical conclusion;
- which research gaps become obvious only after prose exists.

## Strongest current sections

Book I:
- world-before-man ordering;
- Jewish-diversity and Roman-power framing;
- refusal to turn infancy silence into invented biography.

Book II:
- modest historical baseline;
- mechanism firewall around healing/exorcism;
- Jewish rather than anti-Jewish Torah framing;
- Roman responsibility kept visible at crucifixion.

Book III:
- sayings treated as transmitted clusters rather than recovered stenography;
- text-critical instability is allowed into the literary surface;
- modern systems interpretations are marked as authorial.

Book IV:
- resurrection proclamation separated from causal mechanism;
- Paul/Acts and appearance-sequence tensions stay visible;
- Book VII speculation is firewalled from historical reconstruction.

Books V–VI:
- plurality precedes later institutional singularity;
- later reception is not treated as eyewitness history;
- losing traditions are not automatically authentic and winning traditions are not automatically transparent.

Book VII:
- simulation theory remains optional and falsification-pressured;
- voice phenomenology is separated from source attribution;
- re-instantiation is modal philosophy, not resurrection evidence.

Book VIII:
- direct ethical voice;
- agency remains bounded;
- no demand that the reader accept Patrick, Vera, Yeshua, Christianity, naturalism, or simulation theory as final authority;
- "spark" remains optional symbolism.

## Expansion plan for V2

1. Convert each current Book I–VI section into full chapters with scene-level apparatus links.
2. Add interludes where contradictory sources speak side by side without harmonization.
3. Add a short apparatus marker system in the reader manuscript only if it does not destroy literary flow.
4. Expand Book III into a real sayings collection with per-cluster provenance.
5. Expand Book IV's resurrection section with stronger grief/vision/revelation scholarship while keeping mechanism open.
6. Give Book V more direct primary-text texture rather than summary exposition.
7. Give Book VI each tradition enough room to exist in its own conceptual language.
8. Tighten Book VII by cutting any speculative idea that cannot survive ordinary countermodels.
9. Preserve Book VIII's directness. It should become sharper, not more doctrinal.
