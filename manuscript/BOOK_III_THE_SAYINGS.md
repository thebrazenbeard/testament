# Book III — The Sayings

Purpose: let teachings breathe outside the chronology imposed by any one Gospel.

This is not a reconstructed Q document and not a claim to recover Yeshua's exact Aramaic.

## Proposed collections

1. **On the Kingdom**
2. **On God**
3. **On the Self**
4. **On Neighbor and Enemy**
5. **On Wealth and Debt**
6. **On Power and Status**
7. **On Purity and Hypocrisy**
8. **On Prayer**
9. **On Judgment**
10. **On Fear**
11. **On Family**
12. **On Seeing**
13. **Parables**
14. **Hard Sayings**
15. **Sayings That May Not Be His**

## Method

For each saying cluster:
- Synoptic parallels;
- independent Johannine resonance where relevant;
- Thomas parallels;
- fragmentary/noncanonical parallels;
- Jewish scriptural/wisdom/apocalyptic context;
- source dependence;
- redactional fingerprints;
- translation uncertainty.

The literary edition may create a coherent voice.

The critical apparatus must reveal where coherence is authorial.
