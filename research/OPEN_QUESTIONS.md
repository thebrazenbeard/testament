# Open Questions Register

## Biography

- What can responsibly be said about Yeshua's family?
- Was Nazareth stigma or disputed paternity historically operative?
- What is the best reconstruction of his relationship with John?
- How long did his ministry last?
- How much travel outside Galilee is historically defensible?
- What did "kingdom of God" mean across different sayings strata?
- What did he think his own role was?
- What precipitated Roman execution?

## Teaching

- Which sayings have plausible multiple/independent attestation?
- Which sayings are likely community redaction?
- How Jewish were specific disputes later framed as Judaism versus Christianity?
- What did enemy-love, forgiveness, debt, purity, Sabbath, wealth, and family language mean in context?

## Death and resurrection

- What can be established about burial?
- What are the source layers of the empty-tomb traditions?
- What kinds of experiences produced appearance claims?
- How rapidly did resurrection language develop?
- How did James, Peter, Mary Magdalene, and Paul differ?

## Alternative traditions

- What is the earliest recoverable Panthera/Pandera stratum?
- What are the manuscript variants in rabbinic parallels?
- How early are Thomasine sayings?
- Which Jewish-Christian gospel fragments preserve independent tradition?
- What did Celsus actually inherit versus compose?

## Simulation layer

- Could a predictive controller create self-fulfilling interventions?
- Would "divine hiddenness" be expected after intervention contamination?
- Could revelation be modeled as a control-plane side-channel without claiming it happened?
- What observations would distinguish that model from psychology, culture, and coincidence?
- Does the model become unfalsifiable when host capability is unconstrained?

## Ethical thesis

- How do we teach agency without replacing one authority with the author's authority?
- Can the book honor prayer/faith while rejecting passivity?
- How can "divine spark" remain available to theist, mystic, naturalist, and simulation reader without pretending those ontologies are identical?
