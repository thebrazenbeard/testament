# Yeshua Live Questions and Speculative Research Board V1

Status: RESEARCH AGENDA / HYPOTHESIS BOARD / NOT EVIDENCE

## Purpose

Preserve open questions raised in the Testament chat after reviewing the historical Yeshua baseline, Thomas, Mary, Judas, and Panthera traditions.

Questions generate research. They do not promote themselves to evidence.

## Formation and John

- Was Yeshua merely baptized by Yohanan/John, or formed inside John's movement?
- If he began under John, what caused differentiation?
- Did Yeshua's movement baptize while John was still active?
- Does John 3-4 preserve memory of overlapping baptizing movements?

## The Twelve and authority

- Did Yeshua institute a fixed Twelve, or did a symbolic Twelve stabilize later?
- How fixed were membership and function?
- Why do different traditions privilege Peter, James, John, Mary, Judas Iscariot, or Judas Thomas differently?
- Did Yeshua teach materially different things privately and publicly?

## Mission, kingdom, and self-conception

- Was the intended mission primarily to Israel?
- Did Yeshua envision the later gentile mission?
- Did he expect imminent cosmic intervention, present divine reign, social transformation, or a combination?
- What did Yeshua think he was: prophet, messiah, Son of Man, royal agent, wisdom teacher, divine agent, or something not captured by later categories?
- How much does later Christology reorganize earlier remembered teaching around the identity of the teacher?

## Mary and Judas

- Was Mary Magdalene more important during the historical ministry than later canonical narrative proportions suggest?
- Do later Mary/Peter conflicts preserve any memory of first-generation authority competition?
- What was Judas Iscariot's actual motive?
- Was betrayal driven by money, disillusionment, political calculation, attempted provocation, cooperation with authorities, or something unrecoverable?
- Did Yeshua anticipate Judas's action?
- Is there early evidence for unusual Judas intimacy beyond membership in the Twelve?

## Marriage, family, and missing years

- Was Yeshua married? Current evidence establishes neither a wife nor a complete lifelong-celibacy biography.
- What happened between childhood and the John horizon?
- What did tekton mean in his household economy?
- Did he work beyond Nazareth?
- What languages could he speak/read?
- How much formal or oral scriptural education did he have?
- Was his paternity socially disputed?
- What, if anything, lies behind Panthera/Pandera countertraditions?

## Economics and social program

- How radical was Yeshua's wealth teaching in practice?
- Did "sell what you have" belong to an itinerant movement expecting imminent transformation, a general ethic, or both?
- How central were debt, patronage, subsistence, land, taxation, and status reversal?

## Temple, death, and final meal

- Was the Temple action protest against commerce, prophetic judgment, anti-sacrificial symbolism, anti-priestly critique, eschatological sign, or a later expansion of a smaller disturbance?
- Did the Temple action provide the decisive causal bridge to Roman execution?
- Did Yeshua expect to die, merely recognize danger, or predict a detailed Passion?
- How much of the detailed trial narrative can be reconstructed historically?
- Was the final meal historically a Passover meal?
- Did Yeshua himself interpret bread/wine as his body/blood and impending death?
- How should Paul's earlier meal tradition relate to later narrative Gospels?

## Burial and resurrection

- How early and central was a specific tomb tradition?
- Did resurrection conviction begin with appearance/vision experiences, an empty-tomb discovery, both, or distinct streams that later converged?
- What kinds of experiences transformed an executed movement into resurrection proclamation?

## Largest framing question

Did Christianity preserve Yeshua's organizing message, or preserve major pieces of it while shifting the organizing center from God's reign / transformation / seeking / doing toward Yeshua's identity / death / resurrection / correct belief about him?

This is a research program, not a predetermined thesis.

Test by:
- earliest recoverable message clusters;
- Paul, Synoptics, John, Thomas, Mary, Judas, Jewish-Christian fragments, and early reception;
- literary dependence;
- biography vs authority/reception claims;
- genuine discontinuity where found.
