# Source Priority and Research Order

This is a research order, not a hierarchy of spiritual worth.

## Tier 1 — indispensable before sustained biography

1. Undisputed Paul.
2. Mark.
3. Matthew and Luke-Acts with source/dependence analysis.
4. John with independent-tradition and theological-development questions separated.
5. Josephus.
6. John the Baptist context.
7. Dead Sea Scrolls / Second Temple environment.
8. Hebrew Bible and Septuagint passages demonstrably active in early Jesus interpretation.
9. Roman/Judean political chronology and Pilate.
10. material culture of Galilee/Judea.

## Tier 2 — indispensable before writing reception history

- Didache;
- 1 Clement;
- Ignatius;
- Papias;
- Justin;
- Marcion reconstruction;
- Irenaeus;
- Gospel of Thomas;
- Gospel of Peter;
- Jewish-Christian gospel fragments;
- Nag Hammadi Jesus traditions;
- Celsus/Origen;
- early rabbinic parallels.

## Tier 3 — indispensable before final theological synthesis

- Syriac traditions;
- Qur'anic Jesus;
- early Islamic exegesis;
- Hekhalot and Jewish mysticism;
- medieval Kabbalah;
- later Christian mystical traditions;
- comparative philosophy of divine immanence and agency.

## Rule

A later text can be central to the book's meaning while remaining weak evidence for Yeshua's biography.

Importance and historical priority are different axes.
