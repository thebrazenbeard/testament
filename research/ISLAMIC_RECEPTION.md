# Islamic Jesus / Isa Research Lane

## Scope

Islamic sources belong centrally in Testament's reception history because they preserve a major alternative monotheistic interpretation of Jesus and Mary.

They do not become first-century witnesses by theological importance.

## Qur'anic themes to map

- Maryam/Mary;
- miraculous conception;
- Isa as Messiah;
- word/sign/spirit language;
- miracles by God's permission;
- disciples;
- rejection of divine sonship;
- crucifixion language in Qur'an 4:157–158;
- eschatological implications;
- continuity/correction of earlier revelation.

## Source controls

Distinguish:
- Qur'anic text;
- early manuscript witness;
- qira'at/readings;
- tafsir;
- hadith;
- prophetic stories;
- later theology;
- Sufi/mystical Jesus traditions;
- modern apologetic harmonization.

## Research gateway

Corpus Coranicum provides early manuscript cataloging, codicology, paleography, and textual resources:
https://corpuscoranicum.de/en/manuscripts

## Literary rule

Isa can appear in Book VI as a later reception of Yeshua, not as a disguised first-century biography inserted into Book II.
