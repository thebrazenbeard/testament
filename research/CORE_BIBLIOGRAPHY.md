## Christian apocrypha / Nag Hammadi

Use NASSCAL bibliographies to drive text-specific scholarship rather than relying on popular "lost gospel" books.

Priority scholarly names include Tony Burke, J. K. Elliott, and text-specific Coptic/Nag Hammadi editors.

## Simulation / philosophy of mind

Read as philosophy, not religious evidence:
- Nick Bostrom — simulation argument;
- David Chalmers — virtual reality and metaphysics;
- predictive-processing literature;
- personal identity / duplication / continuity literature;
- computationalism and critiques;
- emergence and substrate-independence arguments.

## Cognitive / experiential research

Research domains:
- auditory verbal hallucinations;
- nonclinical voice hearing;
- predictive processing and perception;
- religious experience;
- temporal-order judgments;
- agency and confabulation;
- grief/vision experiences;
- collective memory;
- self-fulfilling prophecy;
- control-system intervention effects.

Clinical findings must not be used to diagnose historical individuals.
