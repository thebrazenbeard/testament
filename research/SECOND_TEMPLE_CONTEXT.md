# Second Temple Context Requirements

Yeshua must be written inside Jewish history, not as a Christian dropped backward into Judaism.

## Required context lanes

### Temple
- priestly hierarchy;
- sacrifice;
- pilgrimage;
- purity;
- Temple economy;
- symbolic and political centrality;
- Herodian rebuilding;
- Roman oversight.

### Torah and interpretation
- legal plurality;
- purity practice;
- Sabbath;
- divorce;
- food;
- vows;
- tithes;
- debt;
- neighbor/enemy ethics;
- scriptural interpretation.

### Groups and movements
Avoid simplistic four-sect cartoons.

Research:
- Pharisees;
- Sadducees;
- Essenes and Qumran relation questions;
- priestly/aristocratic groups;
- scribes;
- revolutionary/bandit/royal claimants;
- baptist/renewal movements;
- apocalyptic and wisdom traditions;
- ordinary village religion not reducible to named sects.

### Apocalypticism
Research:
- resurrection;
- judgment;
- Son of Man traditions;
- angels/demons;
- messianic figures;
- restoration of Israel;
- new covenant;
- cosmic conflict;
- divine kingship.

### Economy and social life
- taxation;
- land tenure;
- debt;
- patronage;
- household structure;
- slavery;
- day labor;
- gender;
- widow/orphan vulnerability;
- village and city differences.

## Dead Sea Scrolls rule

Qumran material illuminates a possible Jewish environment and spectrum of concepts. It is not a secret Gospel of Jesus and does not prove direct contact with Yeshua.
