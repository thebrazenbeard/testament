# Mysticism and Kabbalah Lane

## Why it belongs

The project is interested in divine immanence, hidden structure, revelation, interior transformation, ascent, emanation, fragmentation, repair, and the "spark" metaphor.

Jewish mystical traditions provide major later conceptual worlds for these questions.

## Chronology firewall

Do not retroject medieval Kabbalah into first-century Galilee.

Separate:
- Second Temple apocalyptic ascent;
- early rabbinic mystical material;
- Hekhalot/Merkavah complexes;
- Sefer Yetzirah;
- Bahir;
- Zohar;
- later Kabbalistic systems;
- Lurianic concepts such as divine sparks and repair.

## Comparative opportunities

Possible symbolic comparisons:
- divine image / indwelling;
- Spirit;
- kingdom within/among;
- Logos;
- emanation;
- sefirotic mediation;
- sparks;
- tikkun/repair;
- hidden and revealed God.

Every comparison must state whether it is:
- historical transmission;
- later reinterpretation;
- structural parallel;
- authorial symbolic synthesis.

## Purpose

The mystical layer can enrich the final Testament of the Spark without pretending Yeshua taught medieval Kabbalah.
