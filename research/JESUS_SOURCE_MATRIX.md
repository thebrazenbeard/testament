# Jesus / Yeshua Source Matrix

This matrix states what a source can potentially tell us before deciding whether its claims are true.

| Source family | Approximate source horizon | Best use | Major limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Undisputed Paul | c. 50s CE | earliest extant Christian letters; movement structure; death/resurrection proclamation; known leaders | sparse biography; theological/pastoral aims |
| Mark | c. late 60s–70s | earliest extant narrative Gospel in common scholarly dating; ministry structure and traditions | literary theology; sources mostly inaccessible |
| Matthew | c. 80s–90s | Jewish scriptural framing; sayings; community conflict; infancy tradition | substantial dependence on Mark; redaction |
| Luke-Acts | c. 80s–90s or broader debated range | sayings, infancy, social themes, movement narrative | dependence/source questions; literary historiography |
| John | late 1st / early 2nd century final form | independent traditions possible; Judean material; developed Christology | long discourses and theology require special caution |
| Josephus | 70s–90s CE works | political/religious context; John; James; Jesus passage textual problem | elite retrospective historiography; later Christian transmission issues for Testimonium |
| Thomas | composition debated; Coptic witness later, Greek fragments earlier | sayings comparison; reception; possible early units | collection history uncertain; dependence debated |
| Peter | 2nd century commonly | passion/resurrection reception | later theological development |
| Jewish-Christian gospel fragments | mostly preserved by later authors | alternative early Christian memories | fragmentary; hostile/late preservation |
| Celsus via Origen | late 2nd source preserved 3rd century | hostile countertradition; Panthera accusation; perception of Christians | lost original; mediated by opponent |
| Rabbinic Yeshu/Pandera material | later redaction with debated strata | Jewish counter-memory and polemic | chronology/identity difficult; not contemporary |
| Qur'an | early 7th century | Islamic Jesus/Mary theology and late antique reception | centuries after Yeshua; not contemporary biography |
| Archaeology/material culture | contemporary objects/sites vary | context, plausibility, institutions, naming, practice | rarely identifies narrative individuals directly |

## Use rule

No source is globally "reliable" or "unreliable."

Reliability is proposition-specific:
- textual occurrence;
- source's own belief;
- external event;
- chronology;
- identity;
- causal explanation;
- theology.

Each requires a separate judgment.
