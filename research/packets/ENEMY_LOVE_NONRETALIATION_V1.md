# Enemy Love and Non-Retaliation V1

Status: SAYING_PACKET / REVIEW_REQUIRED

## Primary forms

Priority:
- Matthew 5:38–48;
- Luke 6:27–36;
- Didache 1.3–5.

Matthew and Luke overlap substantially but arrange and formulate the material differently.

## Source history

Traditional Q reconstruction often places enemy love and non-retaliation in shared sayings material.

Michael Labahn's study treats the enemy-love command in relation to Jesus' kingdom proclamation and its reception in Q.

Q remains hypothetical under competing Synoptic solutions, so Testament should preserve the more basic proposition:
Matthew and Luke share an early enemy-love/non-retaliation tradition whose exact literary route is disputed.

## Matthew's antitheses

Matthew frames the material through "you have heard... but I say" structures.

Do not assume every antithetical frame belongs to Yeshua rather than Matthew's composition.

The phrase "hate your enemy" is not a quotation of Leviticus 19:18.

## Luke

Luke integrates:
- love enemies;
- do good;
- bless;
- pray;
- cheek/garment;
- giving;
- reciprocity;
- divine mercy.

The Lukan arrangement may preserve a different ordering of shared material rather than a verbatim alternate sermon transcript.

## Ethical meaning

David Gill's comparison with Socratic non-retaliation highlights that the Gospel forms move beyond simply refusing revenge toward positive concern for enemies, with divine generosity/mercy functioning as rationale.

Reference:
- David Gill, "Socrates and Jesus on Non-Retaliation and Love of Enemies":
  https://doi.org/10.1017/S0360966900025147

## Historical ceiling

Strong at tradition level:
- enemy-love/non-retaliation teaching is deeply embedded in early Synoptic Jesus tradition.

Unresolved:
- exact original cluster;
- exact examples;
- whether every Matthean/Lukan extension belongs to the historical speaker;
- social scope of "enemy" in each layer.

## Testament interpretation

This cluster is central to Book VIII because it attacks retaliatory causal loops.

But the authored systems reading:
"enemy love interrupts a recursive retaliation process"
is ours.

It must not be retrojected as Yeshua's explicit control-theory language.
