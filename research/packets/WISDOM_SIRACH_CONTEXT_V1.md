## Why wisdom belongs in the Jesus reconstruction

Wisdom teaching was not a secular side genre detached from Torah and theology.

Early Jewish wisdom traditions address:
- creation;
- virtue;
- speech;
- wealth;
- friendship;
- justice;
- suffering;
- death;
- revelation;
- Torah;
- personified Wisdom.

C. D. Elledge argues that Ben Sira and Wisdom of Solomon are essential context for Historical Jesus, Q, John and Paul.

Reference:
- "The Glory of Wisdom":
  https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780190274580.003.0002

## Ben Sira

Ben Sira was a Jerusalem sage in the early second century BCE.

Substantial portions survive in Hebrew alongside Greek and other witnesses.

Matthew Goff's 2024 introduction emphasizes:
- finances;
- relationships;
- personal responsibility;
- Torah;
- wisdom;
- pedagogy.

Reference:
- "Find a Great Teacher like Me: The Book of Sirach":
  https://academic.oup.com/book/57955/chapter-abstract/475918602

Jeremy Corley's Oxford handbook treatment additionally highlights:
- honor;
- generosity;
- friendship;
- social justice;
- testing;
- New Testament parallels.

Reference:
- https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordhb/9780190689643.013.15

By the period of Sirach/Wisdom, wisdom and Torah are increasingly intertwined.

## Wisdom of Solomon

Wisdom of Solomon is a Greek Jewish work with strong interaction between Jewish tradition and Hellenistic intellectual categories.

Its earliest surviving reception is heavily Christian, which must not erase its Jewish origin.

Reference:
- "The Christian use and the Jewish origins of the Wisdom of Solomon":
  https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/wisdom-in-ancient-israel/christian-use-and-the-jewish-origins-of-the-wisdom-of-solomon/20AC9BC28C5E91286AC102563A90E49C

## Jesus tradition

Possible wisdom dimensions include:
- aphorisms;
- parables;
- reversal sayings;
- speech ethics;
- wealth;
- friendship/enemy;
- divine Wisdom motifs;
- "Wisdom is justified..." traditions;
- later identification of Yeshua with Wisdom/Logos.

## Guard

"Jesus was a wisdom teacher" and "Jesus was an apocalyptic prophet" need not be mutually exclusive.

Source clusters may carry both.

## Testament consequence

Book III should let Yeshua sound like a Jewish teacher as well as an apocalyptic prophet.

Book V can then show how communities turn wisdom motifs into Christology.
