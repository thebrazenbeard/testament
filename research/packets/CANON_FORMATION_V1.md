# New Testament Canon Formation V1

Status: RESEARCH_PACKET / REVIEW_REQUIRED

## Core correction

The twenty-seven-book New Testament did not fall out of the first century as a closed object.

Collections, citations, contested books and regional practice developed over centuries.

## Second-century collections

A 2026 Oxford Handbook chapter summarizes evidence that:
- a fourfold Gospel and Pauline-letter collections circulated widely by the second century;
- Acts and Revelation are attested in second-century authors;
- Catholic Epistles emerge as a collection later;
- dispute and regional variation continue into the fourth century.

Reference:
- "The Formation of the Christian Canon":
  https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61539/chapter-abstract/537116629

## Canon lists

Gallagher and Meade stress that ancient canon lists require source-specific interpretation and that the evidence is more complex than popular accounts suggest.

Reference:
- *The Biblical Canon Lists from Early Christianity*:
  https://academic.oup.com/book/25501

## Catalysts are not single causes

Marcion, theological competition and prophetic movements such as Montanism affected debates over authoritative texts.

That does not mean one opponent single-handedly caused the canon.

Reference:
- John M. Court, "The Growth of the New Testament":
  https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordhb/9780199237777.003.0030

## Apostolic Fathers warning

The Apostolic Fathers are important evidence for reception and citation, but Paul Foster cautions that their loose citation practices and later surviving manuscripts make them difficult tools for reconstructing an exact second-century New Testament text.

Reference:
- "The Text of the New Testament in the Apostolic Fathers":
  https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780199566365.003.0016

## Manuscript consequence

Book V should show canon as an outcome of use, argument, copying, institutional authority, theological boundary-making and survival.

"Excluded from the Bible" does not itself tell the reader why a text was excluded or how early it was.
