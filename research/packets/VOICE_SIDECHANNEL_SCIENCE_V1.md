# Voice, Side-Channels, and Physical/Cognitive Models V1

Status: CROSS-DISCIPLINARY RESEARCH / SPECULATIVE_MODEL FIREWALL

## Question

When a person sincerely reports hearing a voice with no obvious external speaker, what classes of mechanism are already known before a control-system hypothesis is introduced?

## Voice hearing is not one diagnostic category

A large review of auditory verbal hallucinations reports voice-hearing phenomena in both clinical and non-clinical populations and describes substantial phenomenological overlap.

Reference:
- Johns et al., "A comprehensive review of auditory verbal hallucinations":
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3712258/

Therefore:
voice report != psychotic disorder by definition.

Clinical interpretation requires context, distress, function and other evidence.

Testament must not diagnose historical prophets from texts.

## Physical internally localized sound

The microwave auditory/Frey effect is a real experimentally studied phenomenon in which sufficiently intense pulsed RF energy can produce internally perceived auditory sensations.

A modern review describes reported percepts such as clicks, buzzes, hisses, knocks and chirps and reviews the physical literature.

Reference:
- "Review of Audiovestibular Symptoms Following Exposure to Acoustic and Electromagnetic Energy Outside Conventional Human Hearing":
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7199630/

Current physical explanation centers on thermoacoustically generated pressure waves rather than an ordinary dental filling acting as an AM/FM crystal radio.

A later review notes that brief intense RF pulses can elicit the microwave auditory effect while treating practical weaponization claims cautiously.

Reference:
- Foster & Balzano, "Can the Microwave Auditory Effect Be Weaponized?":
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8733248/

## Side-channel analogy

This gives Testament one narrow but legitimate philosophical observation:

"No obvious external acoustic source" does not logically entail "no external physical cause."

Known physics already contains unusual pathways from environmental energy to internally localized auditory perception.

That fact does NOT establish:
- radio reception as explanation for ordinary voice hearing;
- hidden transmitters;
- divine communication;
- simulation controllers.

## Cultural decoding

- angel;
- demon;
- ancestor;
- spirit;

The ontology assigned to the experience is a second question after the experience itself.

## Control-system hypothesis

Testament's speculative model proposes:

controller predicts action X -> corrective signal enters agent -> agent consciously renders it as a voice/command -> intervention alters behavior -> X becomes more likely or takes a new path.

This does not require backward time travel if the controller can predict/branch faster than simulated time.

## Necessary countermodels

Always retain:
- source-monitoring errors;
- predictive-processing accounts;
- sleep transitions;
- bereavement;
- stress;
- neurological events;
- drugs/toxins;
- cultural reinforcement;
- deliberate deception;
- unknown mechanism.

## Manuscript consequence

Book VII can argue that "voice" names a phenomenology, not a proven source.

The control-system interpretation remains an optional model unless discriminating evidence exists.
