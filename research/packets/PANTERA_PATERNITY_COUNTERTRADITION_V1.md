## Two questions that must not be collapsed

A. Did ancient opponents preserve or construct a tradition that Yeshua's biological father was called Panthera/Pandera?

Yes, such a countertradition exists.

B. Was that father specifically the epigraphically attested soldier Tiberius Julius Abdes Pantera?

Current Testament status: UNESTABLISHED / WEAK.

A similar name plus broad chronological or geographic plausibility is not an identity chain.

## Tiberius Julius Abdes Pantera

A real Roman auxiliary soldier named Tiberius Julius Abdes Pantera is known epigraphically from a tombstone found at Bingen.

Facts about that soldier do not establish:
- presence in Nazareth at the relevant conception window;
- contact with Miriam/Mary;
- sexual contact;
- coercion;
- biological paternity;
- connection to Joseph;
- connection to a first-century stigma tradition.

The soldier and later countertradition remain separate nodes unless a real bridge is found.
