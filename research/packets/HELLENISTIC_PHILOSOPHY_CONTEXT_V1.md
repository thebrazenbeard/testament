# Hellenistic Philosophy Context V1

Status: COMPARATIVE_CONTEXT_PACKET / REVIEW_REQUIRED

## Scope

Relevant philosophical traditions include:
- Stoicism;
- Middle Platonism;
- Cynic traditions;
- Epicureanism;
- popular moral philosophy;
- philosophical Judaism, especially Philo.

These matter much more for the Greek-speaking Jesus movement and later Christian authors than for proving Yeshua personally studied philosophy.

## Stoic / Christian ethical parallels

Research identifies real parallels in:
- moral formation;
- self-mastery;
- social ethics;
- non-retaliation;
- care for others;
- exemplary figures.

Reference:
- *Roman Christianity and Roman Stoicism: A Comparative Study of Ancient Morality*:
  https://academic.oup.com/book/5424

Similarity does not establish direct dependence.

## Paul

A 2024 study by Stanley Stowers argues that Paul selectively adapts both Stoic and Platonic ideas/language rather than fitting neatly into one philosophical system.

Reference:
- "The Dilemma of Paul's Physics":
  https://academic.oup.com/edinburgh-scholarship-online/book/58452/chapter-abstract/484814291

This is particularly important for:
- pneuma;
- body;
- transformation;
- cosmology;
- ethics.

## Christian philosophy develops later

Greek philosophical schools profoundly shape later Christian conceptual language while Christian theology also reshapes philosophy.

Reference:
- "Christian Philosophy," *Oxford Handbook of Early Christian Studies*:
  https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/42623/chapter-abstract/357712939

## Cynic-Jesus caution

Older historical-Jesus models sometimes emphasized Yeshua as a Jewish Cynic-like sage.

Testament may compare:
- itinerancy;
- possessions;
- aphorism;
- social critique;
without assuming direct Cynic schooling.

## Testament consequence

Book III can compare sayings to wider Mediterranean moral discourse.

Book V should show how Greek-speaking Christians acquire philosophical vocabulary for explaining a Jewish Messiah.

Never back-project second-century metaphysics into Galilean conversations without evidence.
