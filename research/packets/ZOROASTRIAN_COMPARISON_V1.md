# Zoroastrian / Iranian Comparison V1

Status: COMPARATIVE_CONTEXT_PACKET / REVIEW_REQUIRED

## Why this appears in comparative religion

Scholars have long discussed possible Iranian/Zoroastrian contributions or parallels to Jewish and Christian ideas such as:
- cosmic dualism;
- angels/demons;
- final judgment;
- resurrection;
- eschatological renovation;
- heaven/hell traditions.

The historical-contact setting under Persian rule makes influence possible.

Possible does not mean demonstrated for every motif.

## Apocalypticism

The Oxford Handbook of Apocalyptic Literature includes Zoroastrianism among the factors debated in explaining the rise of Jewish/Christian apocalyptic thought, alongside internal Israelite prophetic/mythic developments.

Reference:
- Stephen L. Cook, "Apocalyptic Prophecy":
  https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/34434/chapter-abstract/292220228

## Resurrection control

C. D. Elledge's focused history of resurrection shows resurrection developing gradually and diversely within early Jewish texts from around the second century BCE onward.

Reference:
- *Resurrection of the Dead in Early Judaism, 200 BCE–CE 200*:
  https://academic.oup.com/book/3161

This prevents the simplistic inference:
"resurrection exists in Iran, therefore Judaism copied it."

Internal Jewish scriptural, theodicy and creation arguments also matter.

## Mithras warning

Iranian Mithra and Roman Mithras are related historically at some level but are not identical cult systems.

Modern scholarship rejected the old model of Roman Mithraism as a straightforward Persian religion imported intact.

Reference:
- Roger Beck, "Mithras":
  https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61673/chapter-abstract/549651878

## Testament consequence

Book VI/VII may compare cosmic-repair, judgment and resurrection systems.

Every proposed Zoroastrian -> Jewish/Christian transmission edge must identify:
- plausible date;
- contact route;
- direction;
- motif specificity;
- independent internal development alternatives.

SIMILARITY DOES NOT EQUAL TRANSMISSION.
