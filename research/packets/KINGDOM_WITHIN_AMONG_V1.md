# Kingdom "Within" / "Among" V1

Status: SAYING_PACKET / REVIEW_REQUIRED

## Primary text

Luke 17:20–21 says the kingdom does not come in an observable way such that people can point "here" or "there," followed by the difficult phrase:

*he basileia tou theou entos hymon estin.*

## Translation dispute

The central problem is *entos hymon*.

Major interpretive options include:
- "within you";
- "among you";
- "in your midst";
- less commonly, "within your reach/power."

The grammar alone has not eliminated the debate.

A classic Harvard Theological Review study documents the long history of both internal and communal/present interpretations and notes that the Gospel of Thomas receives a related saying explicitly as an interior/exterior kingdom claim.

Reference:
- Colin H. Roberts, "The Kingdom of Heaven (Lk. XVII.21)":
  https://doi.org/10.1017/S0017816000019313

## Thomas

Thomas 3 says the kingdom is "inside of you and outside of you" in the surviving Coptic tradition; Thomas 113 says the kingdom is spread upon the earth but people do not see it.

Cambridge's modern introduction provides Thomas 3 with Luke 17:20–21 as a canonical parallel.

Reference:
- Delbert Burkett, "Selections from the Gospel of Thomas":
  https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/an-introduction-to-the-new-testament-and-the-origins-of-christianity/32A6751C4FE08D443DC4BDD934A3C343/selections-from-the-gospel-of-thomas/C305AC18DFB56418C0E792BA1E0501DF

Thomas's development cannot automatically determine Luke's earlier Greek semantics.

## Luke's theology

Modern scholarship resists older claims that Luke simply replaces imminent eschatology with a long church age.

Reference:
- Klaus Haacker, "Der Geist und das Reich im Lukanischen Werk":
  https://doi.org/10.1017/S0028688512000392

So "among you" should not be used as a shortcut to eliminate future eschatology, nor should "within you" eliminate social/apocalyptic dimensions.

## Historical ceiling

Strong:
- Luke preserves a saying denying simplistic observable localization of kingdom arrival.

Unresolved:
- exact force of *entos*;
- direct relationship to Thomas 3/113;
- whether the Lukan form is historical Yeshua wording or Lukan tradition/redaction.

## Testament consequence

This is one of the most important phrases for the project's agency thesis, which makes it especially dangerous.

Book III must preserve the translation dispute.

Book VIII may say "the kingdom within" as our deliberate literary/theological inheritance, but must not claim lexical certainty from Luke 17:21.
