# Isaiah 61 / Good News / Release V1

Status: SCRIPTURAL_INTERTEXT_PACKET / REVIEW_REQUIRED

## Source

Isaiah 61 speaks in the voice of an anointed herald:
- good news to poor;
- binding/healing;
- liberty/release;
- divine favor;
- restoration.

Luke 4 places a composite Isaiah citation into Yeshua's Nazareth synagogue scene and uses it programmatically for Luke-Acts.

## Historical questions

Separate:
1. Yeshua used Isaiah 61 traditions;
2. Yeshua spoke this exact composite citation in Nazareth;
3. Luke constructed the scene from Yeshua tradition plus scriptural program;
4. Jubilee/release economics are part of the allusion.

These propositions have different evidence.

## Broader Isaianic reception

The Oxford Handbook of Isaiah notes that New Testament authors repeatedly use Isaiah as a framework for:
- good news;
- healing;
- opposition;
- new creation;
- mission.

Reference:
- https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordhb/9780190669249.013.30

## Release vocabulary

Testament should research Greek *aphesis* across:
- release/liberation;
- debt;
- forgiveness;
- Jubilee-related traditions;
before choosing one English gloss.

## Manuscript consequence

Book II may associate Yeshua's mission with poor/release/healing Isaianic tradition.

It should not treat Luke 4 as stenographic evidence of one historical synagogue reading unless source analysis earns that conclusion.
