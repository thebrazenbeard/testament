# Judge Not / Measure / Speck and Log V1

Status: SAYING_PACKET / WITNESS_CONTROL_ACTIVE / REVIEW_REQUIRED

## Primary forms

- Matthew 7:1–5;
- Luke 6:37–42;
- Mark 4:24 as a related "measure" saying;
- Gospel of Thomas 26 as a related speck/log saying.

## Cluster warning

These sayings appear together in Matthew/Luke but not necessarily as one original speech.

Possible components:
- do not judge/condemn;
- forgive/release;
- measure reciprocity;
- blind guide;
- disciple/teacher;
- speck/log.

Luke's arrangement contains material not all paralleled in Matthew at the same position.

## Semantic questions

"Judge" can mean:
- evaluate;
- condemn;
- render a legal/moral judgment.

The saying cannot honestly be reduced to:
"never evaluate anyone."

The immediate hypocrisy/self-examination imagery complicates that reading.

## Measure

The reciprocal measure saying has parallels in Mark's different literary context, warning against simply treating the Matthean/Lukan sermon location as original.

## Thomas

Thomas 26 preserves the speck/log image without the full Matthean/Lukan discourse.

Its relation to Synoptic forms remains a saying-level dependence question, not automatic independent attestation.

P.Oxy. I 1:
- Greek papyrus codex leaf;
- Bodleian Library MS. Gr. th. e 7 (P);
- catalogued by NASSCAL as second century;
- preserves Thomas 26–30, 77b, 31–33.

The leaf begins within logion 26, so it directly witnesses only part of the saying.

Nag Hammadi Codex II preserves the complete Coptic Thomas 26.

That creates a simple but important rule:

**missing Greek wording is not manuscript evidence merely because the Coptic text can supply a plausible completion.**

### Cluster control

Thomas 26 preserves the speck/log image.

It does not preserve the full Matthean/Lukan complex of:
- judge / condemn;
- forgive/release;
- reciprocal measure;
- blind guide;
- disciple/teacher;
- speck/log.

Mark also places a related measure saying elsewhere.

That distribution blocks a simple reconstruction in which every element belonged to one original sermon sequence.

### Semantic guard

The project cannot reduce the cluster to:

> never evaluate anyone.

The extant forms keep several live semantic dimensions:
- condemnation;
- reciprocity;
- hypocrisy;
- self-examination;
- moral/legal judgment.

Book VIII may distinguish discernment from condemnation as authored ethical synthesis.

Book III must not pretend the ancient wording already supplies that modern distinction cleanly.

### Current historical ceiling

Established:
- Thomas 26 is directly extant in fragmentary Greek P.Oxy. I 1 and complete Coptic NHC II;
- the speck/log saying circulates outside the full Matthean/Lukan cluster;
- the Synoptic cluster is compositionally complex enough that one-speech reconstruction requires argument.

Not established:
- Thomasine independence;
- one original cluster order;
- complete Greek Thomas 26 wording from the surviving papyrus;
- one exhaustive lexical meaning for "judge."

## Historical ceiling

Strong:
- reciprocal judgment/self-examination traditions are deeply embedded in early Jesus sayings material.

Unresolved:
- original grouping;
- whether "judge not" referred primarily to condemnation;
- exact Thomas relationship.

## Testament consequence

Book VIII may distinguish discernment from condemnation.

That ethical synthesis is ours; Book III preserves the ancient ambiguity first.
