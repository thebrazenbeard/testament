# Judge Not / Measure / Speck and Log V1

Status: SAYING_PACKET / REVIEW_REQUIRED

## Primary forms

- Matthew 7:1–5;
- Luke 6:37–42;
- Mark 4:24 as a related "measure" saying;
- Gospel of Thomas 26 as a related speck/log saying.

## Cluster warning

These sayings appear together in Matthew/Luke but not necessarily as one original speech.

Possible components:
- do not judge/condemn;
- forgive/release;
- measure reciprocity;
- blind guide;
- disciple/teacher;
- speck/log.

Luke's arrangement contains material not all paralleled in Matthew at the same position.

## Semantic questions

"Judge" can mean:
- evaluate;
- condemn;
- render a legal/moral judgment.

The saying cannot honestly be reduced to:
"never evaluate anyone."

The immediate hypocrisy/self-examination imagery complicates that reading.

## Measure

The reciprocal measure saying has parallels in Mark's different literary context, warning against simply treating the Matthean/Lukan sermon location as original.

## Thomas

Thomas 26 preserves the speck/log image without the full Matthean/Lukan discourse.

Its relation to Synoptic forms remains a saying-level dependence question, not automatic independent attestation.

## Historical ceiling

Strong:
- reciprocal judgment/self-examination traditions are deeply embedded in early Jesus sayings material.

Unresolved:
- original grouping;
- whether "judge not" referred primarily to condemnation;
- exact Thomas relationship.

## Testament consequence

Book VIII may distinguish discernment from condemnation.

That ethical synthesis is ours; Book III preserves the ancient ambiguity first.
