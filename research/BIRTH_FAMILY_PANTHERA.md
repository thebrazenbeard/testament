# Birth, Family, and Panthera Research Contract

## Source families

Must compare:
- Mark's absence of a birth narrative and hometown/family material;
- Matthew's infancy narrative;
- Luke's infancy narrative;
- John and Pauline references relevant to family/ancestry;
- James/brothers traditions;
- Protevangelium and later infancy traditions;
- Celsus via Origen;
- rabbinic Stada/Pandera/Pandeira complexes;
- material attestation of Pantera as a personal name;
- modern scholarly arguments.

## Non-harmonization rule

Matthew and Luke are not automatically combined into one itinerary.

Their genealogies, geography, chronology, and narrative purposes require separate analysis.

## Panthera rule

Current upstream evidence supports:
- Origen reports Celsus's Panthera accusation;
- Origen rejects it;
- Pantera is an attested ancient personal name;
- later rabbinic texts preserve related-looking countertraditions with difficult identity and dating questions.

Current evidence does not establish:
- Panthera as biological father;
- Tiberius Julius Abdes Pantera as that father;
- an original first-century scandal;
- a causal psychological theory of Yeshua.

## Literary possibility

The book may portray uncertainty surrounding parentage only if the specific scene's construction budget admits that it is authored reconstruction.

A provocative hypothesis does not become a childhood memory.
