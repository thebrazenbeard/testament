# Source Universe

Status: MINIMUM COMPREHENSIVE ACQUISITION SCOPE

"Every source" is not a finite honest claim: manuscripts continue to be catalogued, fragments are re-edited, and bibliographies grow. This file defines the source universe that must be checked before the project can claim research saturation.

## A. Hebrew Bible / Israelite-Jewish scriptural strata

Acquire and compare where relevant:
- Masoretic textual tradition;
- Dead Sea Scroll biblical witnesses;
- Septuagint and Jewish Greek revisions;
- Samaritan Pentateuch where Pentateuchal differences matter;
- Aramaic Targum traditions, dated as later witnesses unless earlier strata are independently argued.

Priority books for Yeshua context:
Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy, Samuel, Kings, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Daniel, Psalms, Proverbs, Job, Zechariah, Malachi.

Critical/digital gateways:
- Leon Levy Dead Sea Scrolls Digital Library — https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/
- Göttingen Septuagint project — https://septuaginta.uni-goettingen.de/
- ETCBC/BHSA linguistic corpus — https://github.com/ETCBC/bhsa
- Sefaria library — https://www.sefaria.org/texts

## B. Deuterocanonical / Second Temple Jewish literature

## E. Philo, Josephus, and Jewish-Hellenistic context

Philo:
- creation/law/allegory works;
- Embassy to Gaius;
- Flaccus;
- texts relevant to Logos, divine mediation, law, virtue, and Hellenistic Jewish interpretation.

Josephus:
- Jewish War;
- Jewish Antiquities;
- Life;
- Against Apion.

Priority passages include John the Baptist, James, Jesus/Testimonium questions, Pilate, priesthood, sects, Herodians, revolt context.

Gateway:
- Perseus/Scaife Josephus catalog and Greek text.
- Scholarly modern editions/commentaries should supersede old public-domain translations for close claims.

## F. New Testament

Highest historical priority:
- undisputed Pauline letters;
- Mark;
- Matthew;
- Luke-Acts;
- John;
- Hebrews;
- James;
- Revelation;
- remaining catholic and deutero-Pauline letters for reception/community development.

- INTF New Testament Virtual Manuscript Room — https://ntvmr.uni-muenster.de/

## N. Islamic Jesus traditions

Primary:
- Qur'an, especially Maryam/Isa passages across surahs;
- early Qur'anic manuscripts and textual history;
- early tafsir where Jesus interpretation matters;
- major hadith collections for Jesus/es­chatology traditions;
- prophetic stories and historiography, source-dated;
- Islamic mystical Jesus traditions, source-dated.

Gateway:
- Corpus Coranicum manuscripts — https://corpuscoranicum.de/en/manuscripts

The Qur'an is a seventh-century source about a first-century figure and must not be silently treated as contemporary biography.

## O. Jewish mysticism / Kabbalah

Reception/comparative layer only unless an earlier stratum is independently established:
- apocalyptic ascent traditions;
- Hekhalot/Merkavah corpora;
- Sefer Yetzirah;
- Sefer ha-Bahir;
- Zohar;
- Lurianic developments where "spark" language or divine fragmentation becomes comparatively relevant.

Use for reception, metaphysics, and symbolic comparison—not retroactive biography.
