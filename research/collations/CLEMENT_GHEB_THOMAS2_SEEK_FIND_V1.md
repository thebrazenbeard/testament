# Clement of Alexandria — Gospel according to the Hebrews / Thomas 2 Seeking Chain V1

Status: **DIRECT PRIMARY GREEK CLEMENT TEXT BOUND / LOST-GOSPEL ATTRIBUTION ASYMMETRY PRESERVED / LITERARY DIRECTION UNRESOLVED**

## Scope

This control binds Clement of Alexandria's two Greek *Stromata* passages used to compare the Gospel according to the Hebrews tradition with the seek–find–marvel–reign–rest chain related to Gospel of Thomas 2.

It preserves the difference between:
- what Clement explicitly attributes to the Gospel according to the Hebrews;
- what Clement quotes later without repeating that source name in the immediate sentence;
- what modern scholarship infers about the relation between those two Clementine loci and Thomas 2.

## Stromata 2.9.45.5 — explicit attribution

Direct Greek:

`ᾗ κἀν τῷ καθ' Ἑβραίους εὐαγγελίῳ ὁ θαυμάσας βασιλεύσει γέγραπται καὶ ὁ βασιλεύσας ἀναπαήσεται.`

Evidence class:
`DIRECT_CLEMENT_GREEK_EXPLICIT_GOSPEL_OF_HEBREWS_ATTRIBUTION`

Directly established:
- Clement names `τῷ καθ' Ἑβραίους εὐαγγελίῳ`;
- the attributed saying contains the sequence wonder → reign → rest;
- Clement's surviving text is testimony to a lost/non-extant gospel tradition, not the physical recovery of that gospel.

## Stromata 5.14.96.3 — fuller chain

Direct Greek:

`οὐ παύσεται ὁ ζητῶν, ἕως ἂν εὕρῃ· εὑρὼν δὲ θαμβηθήσεται, θαμβηθεὶς δὲ βασιλεύσει, βασιλεύσας δὲ ἐπαναπαήσεται.`

Sequence:
- seek;
- find;
- marvel/astonishment;
- reign;
- rest.

Evidence class:
`DIRECT_CLEMENT_GREEK_FULLER_CHAIN_WITHOUT_REPEATED_SOURCE_NAME_IN_IMMEDIATE_SENTENCE`

Attribution guard:
- this immediate sentence does **not** freshly repeat `Gospel according to the Hebrews`;
- the relation of this fuller chain to the explicitly attributed 2.9 saying is a contextual/scholarly connection;
- Testament must not silently rewrite the 5.14 sentence as though Clement explicitly reintroduced the lost gospel there.

## Relation to Greek Thomas 2

The reconstructed/published Greek P.Oxy. 654 Thomas 2 chain is very close in sequence:
- seek;
- find;
- marvel/astonishment;
- reign;
- rest.

Important witness boundary:
- P.Oxy. 654 is fragmentary;
- parts of Thomas 2's Greek chain are restored editorially;
- the Clement text is not a manuscript of Thomas;
- the lost Gospel according to the Hebrews is not physically extant in Clement.

Therefore the comparison establishes:
`CLOSE_RELATED_SEEK_FIND_WISDOM_CHAIN`

It does not establish:
- Thomas copied the Gospel according to the Hebrews;
- the Gospel according to the Hebrews copied Thomas;
- both copied one identifiable written source;
- the fuller Clement 5.14 chain is itself explicitly attributed to the Gospel according to the Hebrews by Clement in that sentence;
- one recovered historical saying behind all forms.

## Textual-form guard

The direct Clement sources show minor lexical/form differences across editions/transcriptions, including `θαυμάσας/θαμβηθήσεται`-type forms and `ἀναπαύσεται/ἐπαναπαήσεται`-type forms.

Testament preserves the directly read forms in their own loci rather than normalizing them into one synthetic Clement quotation.

## Historical consequence

Strong:
- an early Christian seek/find/wonder/reign/rest tradition existed outside the surviving Gospel of Thomas manuscript tradition;
- Clement explicitly associates a shorter wonder/reign/rest form with the Gospel according to the Hebrews;
- Clement elsewhere preserves the fuller chain strongly comparable to Greek Thomas 2.

Unresolved:
- exact literary direction;
- whether the fuller chain came from the same lost gospel source in Clement's immediate act of quotation;
- whether Thomas 2 preserves an independent, dependent, or common-tradition form;
- the exact unreconstructed Greek wording of every lacunose portion of P.Oxy. 654 Thomas 2.
