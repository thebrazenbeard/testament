# Greek Line Collation — Gospel of Thomas 3 and 26 V1

Status: CRITICAL_EDITION_LINE_COLLATION_COMPLETE / IMAGE_AUTOPSY_NOT_CLAIMED

Purpose:
- bind the exact published papyrus line ranges underlying Thomas 3 and Thomas 26 controls;
- distinguish extant Greek letters from editorial restoration;
- prevent a reconstructed phrase from being described as continuously extant papyrus text;
- preserve direct Greek evidence without promoting literary dependence beyond the manuscript evidence.

## Editorial basis

Primary manuscript / edition anchors:
- P.Oxy. IV 654 = British Library Papyrus 1531; Gospel of Thomas opening through logion 7.
- P.Oxy. I 1 = Bodleian Library MS. Gr. th. e. 7 (P); Gospel of Thomas 26–30, 77b, 31–33.
- Grenfell and Hunt, *The Oxyrhynchus Papyri* IV (editio princeps of P.Oxy. 654).

- later critical transcriptions cited through NASSCAL.

Notation:
- PAPYRUS_VISIBLE = letters represented in the published diplomatic line.
- EDITORIAL_RESTORATION = words/letters supplied across lacunae by an editor or reconstruction.
- NORMALIZED_TEXT = accents, breathings, spacing, and standard orthography added for comparison.
- No claim of direct Testament image autopsy is made here.

## Thomas 3 — P.Oxy. 654.9–21

Witness:
- WIT-POXY-654-GOSPEL-THOMAS

Relevant diplomatic line sequence in the editio princeps includes:

- line 15: `...ΤΕΣ ΥΜΑΣ ΚΑΙ Η ΒΑΣ[`
- line 16: `ΕΝΤΟΣ ΥΜΩΝ [.]ΣΤΙ [`

The critical reconstruction commonly printed for this transition is approximately:

`...τες ὑμᾶς· καὶ ἡ βασ[ιλεία τοῦ θεοῦ] ἐντὸς ὑμῶν [ἐσ]τι ...`

### Physical / editorial segmentation

PAPYRUS_VISIBLE:
- `καὶ ἡ βασ...`
- `ἐντὸς ὑμῶν`
- enough of `ἐστι` to support the verb with a lacunose/restored letter.

EDITORIAL_RESTORATION:
- completion of `βασ...` to `βασιλεία`;
- `τοῦ θεοῦ` in the lacuna between the visible kingdom stem and `ἐντὸς ὑμῶν`;
- any surrounding wording supplied from the Coptic or editorial reconstruction where the papyrus is broken.

### Consequence

Direct Greek evidence supports:
- a kingdom-expression immediately before `ἐντὸς ὑμῶν`;
- the phrase `ἐντὸς ὑμῶν` itself at the relevant point in Thomas 3.

Direct Greek evidence does NOT by itself support the claim that the complete normalized phrase:

`ἡ βασιλεία τοῦ θεοῦ ἐντὸς ὑμῶν ἐστι`

is continuously extant as ink.

The divine-title completion remains editorial reconstruction.

This distinction is mandatory in:
- VAR-KINGDOM-WITHIN-THOMAS-3-113;
- KINGDOM_WITHIN_AMONG_V1.md;
- all future Luke 17:21 / Thomas 3 dependence claims.

## Thomas 26 — P.Oxy. 1.1–4

Witness:
- WIT-POXY-1-GOSPEL-THOMAS

Published normalized Greek for the surviving conclusion is:

`καὶ τότε διαβλέψεις ἐκβαλεῖν τὸ κάρφος τὸ ἐν τῷ ὀφθαλμῷ τοῦ ἀδελφοῦ σου.`

The clause is localized to:
- P.Oxy. 1 lines 1–4.

### Physical / editorial segmentation

PAPYRUS_VISIBLE / CRITICAL-TEXT CONTINUOUS CLAUSE:
- the concluding Thomas 26 sequence represented above.

No opening or middle of Thomas 26 survives in P.Oxy. 1.

Therefore:
- the direct Greek witness applies only to the concluding clause;
- the full saying's opening and "missing middle" pattern depend on the Coptic NHC II witness plus comparison with Matthew/Luke.

### Synoptic comparison control

Mark Goodacre identifies a thirteen-word verbatim agreement between the P.Oxy. 1 conclusion and Luke 6:42, with the placement of `ἐκβαλεῖν` aligning with Matthew 7:5.

The manuscript fact is:
- a substantial direct Greek verbal overlap in the surviving conclusion.

The literary inference remains separate:
- direct dependence is not established solely by this overlap;
- oral/formulaic stability remains a live countermodel.

## Cross-control result

Thomas 3:
- direct Greek overlap = real;
- divine-title phrase = partly restored;
- do not quote the reconstructed full phrase as continuously extant ink.

Thomas 26:
- direct Greek concluding overlap = real and comparatively continuous;
- opening/middle = not preserved in Greek;
- do not advertise the complete Thomas 26 as a Greek witness.

## Evidence ceiling

Established:
- exact papyrus line ranges for the relevant direct Greek controls;
- restoration boundary for Thomas 3;
- preserved-clause boundary for Thomas 26.

Not established:

- one final critical text beyond published editions;
- direct Thomas-to-Luke or Luke-to-Thomas literary dependence;
