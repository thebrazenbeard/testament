# Irenaeus / Valentinian Lost-Sheep Reception V1

Status: DIRECT_PRIMARY_RECEPTION_TEXT_BOUND / THOMAS_107_DIAGNOSTIC_CONSTRAINT_NOT_FOUND

Date: 2026-09-19

## Question

Does Irenaeus's report of Valentinian/Marcosian lost-sheep interpretation materially constrain the distinctive ending of Gospel of Thomas 107?

Diagnostic Thomas 107 features under review:
- the lost sheep is the largest;
- the shepherd says he loves it more than the ninety-nine.

## Direct Irenaeus evidence

### Against Heresies I.16

Irenaeus reports Marcosian numerological use of the lost-sheep and lost-drachma parables. The straying sheep is mapped into their Aeonic/number system, and the remaining numbers are manipulated toward ninety-nine.

This is direct evidence that a second-century gnosticizing Christian school reused the lost-sheep image as symbolic/numerological reception.

It is not direct evidence for Thomas 107's distinctive valuation ending.

No reviewed I.16 passage supplies:
- a largest sheep;
- a more valuable sheep;
- a shepherd loving the lost sheep more than the ninety-nine.

### Against Heresies II

Irenaeus again attacks the Valentinian use of the lost sheep and the ninety-nine. He reports that the Saviour comes to the lost sheep to transfer it to the right hand / salvation while the ninety-nine remain in the fold, within their symbolic number scheme.

Again, the reviewed passage supplies:
- one lost sheep;
- ninety-nine remaining;
- recovery/salvation symbolism;
- Valentinian numerical/allegorical reuse.

It does not supply the Thomas 107 diagnostic features:
- largest sheep;
- greater value;
- love-more-than-the-ninety-nine ending.

## Comparison with Thomas 107

Thomas 107 remains locally distinctive in the currently bound evidence because its ending gives the lost sheep a valuation rationale:
- it is the largest;
- the shepherd loves it more than the ninety-nine.

Irenaeus's Valentinian/Marcosian material does not demonstrate that this exact Thomasine motif was circulating in the school he describes.

Therefore:
- IRENAEUS_VALENTINIAN_RECEPTION != THOMAS_107_TEXT_WITNESS;
- GNOSTICIZING_LOST_SHEEP_ALLEGORY != EVIDENCE_OF_THOMAS_DEPENDENCE;
- absence of the Thomas-specific ending in Irenaeus is not proof that the motif did not exist;
- presence of one/99 symbolism in Valentinian circles is not enough to label Thomas 107 a Valentinian redaction.

## Materiality decision

For the current Book III reception-expansion gate, this target is:

TRIAGED_NONDIAGNOSTIC_FOR_DISTINCTIVE_THOMAS_107_ENDING.

It is worth retaining as reception history because it shows second-century gnosticizing allegorical use of the lost-sheep complex.

It does not meet the project's medium-value threshold for a new reception control whose purpose is to constrain transmission, wording, dependence, or the distinctive Thomas 107 ending.

No new ECR/PAT ledger control is warranted solely from this evidence.

## Historical ceiling

Established:
- Irenaeus directly reports Valentinian/Marcosian reuse of the lost-sheep / ninety-nine complex.
- that reuse is allegorical and numerological in the passages reviewed.

Not established:
- use of Gospel of Thomas 107 by those groups;
- knowledge of Thomas 107's "largest sheep" wording;
- knowledge of Thomas 107's "love more than the ninety-nine" ending;
- Valentinian authorship or redaction of Thomas 107;
- literary direction between Thomas 107 and the Synoptic forms.

## Sources

Primary:
- Irenaeus, Against Heresies I.16.
- Irenaeus, Against Heresies II, discussion of the lost sheep / ninety-nine in refutation of Valentinian number symbolism.

Comparative:

## Consequence

The lost-sheep medium-value reception candidate is closed as non-diagnostic for the current evidence cut.

Reopen if a direct pre-Nicene source is found that bears specifically on:
- the largest-sheep motif;
- greater-value logic;
- love-more-than-the-ninety-nine wording;
- or a demonstrable Thomas-107 textual/reception link.
