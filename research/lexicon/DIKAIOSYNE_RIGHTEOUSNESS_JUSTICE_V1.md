# Dikaiosynē — Righteousness / Justice V1

Status: LEXICAL_PACKET / REVIEW_REQUIRED

## Problem

English divides "righteousness" (religious/moral virtue) from "justice" (social/legal fairness) more sharply than the biblical semantic field often does.

Greek *dikaiosynē* participates in both Hellenic virtue language and the Septuagint's rendering of Hebrew *tsedaqah* traditions.

## Biblical field

A Cambridge study notes that *dikaiosynē* in Greek Jewish scripture is tied to:
- God's just judgment;
- vindication;
- saving mercy;
- human conduct corresponding to God's will;
- "righteousness and justice" as practiced life.

Reference:

## Matthew high-risk passages

Examples:
- hunger/thirst for *dikaiosynē*;
- persecuted because of *dikaiosynē*;
- "unless your *dikaiosynē* exceeds...";
- seek kingdom and *dikaiosynē*;
- practice/do *dikaiosynē*.

No single English choice fits all without interpretation.

## Translation strategy

Possible:
- righteousness;
- justice;
- right conduct;
- covenantal rightness;
- doing what is right.

Use apparatus notes where the English choice narrows the semantic field.

## Testament consequence

The project should not force a false split between "personal righteousness" and "social justice."

Nor should it turn every occurrence into modern political vocabulary.

Context first.
