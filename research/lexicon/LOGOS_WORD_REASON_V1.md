## Problem

*Logos* is one of the easiest words for Testament's simulation layer to abuse.

**Logos does not mean computer code.**

## Greek semantic field

Depending on context *logos* can carry senses involving:
- word;
- statement;
- account;
- argument;
- discourse;
- reason;
- explanation;
- principle.

## Jewish-Hellenistic background

Philo of Alexandria uses *Logos* language extensively within his account of God, creation, mediation and divine transcendence.

Ronald Williamson's Cambridge treatment emphasizes that even speaking of one single "Philo Logos doctrine" oversimplifies a complex body of usages.

Reference:
