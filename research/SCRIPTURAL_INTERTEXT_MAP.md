# Hebrew Scripture / Septuagint Intertext Map

Status: RESEARCH_ACTIVE

## Purpose

Early Christian writers did not merely quote "prophecies."

They reread Israel's scriptures in light of Yeshua, often:
- typologically;
- midrashically;
- through Septuagint wording;
- through composite citations;
- through narrative imitation;
- through retrospective fulfillment claims.

Testament must separate:
1. original/source-context meaning;
2. pre-Christian Jewish reception;
3. Yeshua-era possible use;
4. early Christian reuse;
5. later doctrinal interpretation.

## Anti-prooftext rule

A Gospel saying "this happened to fulfill X" establishes:
EARLY_CHRISTIAN_INTERPRETATION_OF_X.

It does not by itself establish:
ORIGINAL_AUTHOR_OF_X_PREDICTED_YESHUA.

That historical/theological claim requires separate argument.
