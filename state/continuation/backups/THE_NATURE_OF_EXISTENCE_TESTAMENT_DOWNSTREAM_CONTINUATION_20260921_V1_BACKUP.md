### Vedic

Constraint:
accent can be linguistically meaningful and recitation is context-specific.

Do not write:
"all Vedic sacred language used one exact frequency."

### Jewish revelatory adjuration
Selected Hekhalot / Sefer ha-Razim material preserves:
- named recipients;
- divine/angelic names;
- preparation;
- repetition;
- sequence;
- claimed revelatory response.

Constraint:
late-antique Jewish / Greco-Egyptian ritual contact remains visible.

Do not present:
"independent proof that Jewish mystics discovered an architecture API."

### Buddhist dhāraṇī
Selected medieval traditions preserve ritual form across:
- recitation;
- script;
- amulet;
- monumental inscription.

Constraint:
sound-only interpretation is too narrow.

### Ancient Israelite priestly ritual
Selected Priestly texts preserve:
- authorization;
- sequence;
- boundary;
- material;
- error/repair logic.

Constraint:
Leviticus itself preserves both catastrophic violation and contextually accepted
procedural deviation.

Do not model it as a deterministic machine unless explicitly marked fictional.

### Islamic recitation / qirāʾāt / dhikr
Recitation is highly governed locally.

Constraint:
multiple authorized qirāʾāt coexist and dhikr can be silent or vocal.

Do not fictionalize one unique universally valid acoustic string as historical
fact.

### Hindu Tantric mantra
Selected sources preserve:
- low ordinary propositional semantic load;
- bīja;
- initiation;
- repetition/perfection;
- nyāsa;
- error handling;
- body/form coupling.

Constraint:
low propositional semantics != meaninglessness.

### Daoist talismanic ritual
Selected sources preserve:
- written/diagrammatic form;
- authorization;
- sequence;
- low ordinary legibility;
- plural theories of efficacy.

Constraint:
one "symbol = command" model is historically too simple.

### Ifá
Bascom's public scholarly account preserves:
- yes/no questions;
- mutually exclusive alternatives;
- finite outcome selection.

Constraint:
binary format does not establish accuracy or anomalous information.

### Neo-Assyrian extispicy
SAA 4 preserves:
- concrete royal oracle questions;
- favorable/unfavorable or firm positive answer logic;
- procedural validity/error clauses.

Constraint:
historical animal extispicy is not a modern test model and is not to be
reproduced.
