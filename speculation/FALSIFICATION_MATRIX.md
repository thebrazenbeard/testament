# Speculative Model Falsification Matrix

The purpose of this file is to stop interesting metaphors from becoming self-sealing explanations.

| Hypothesis | What it tries to explain | Ordinary alternatives | Discriminator needed | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GOD_AS_ENVIRONMENT | why "God" is described as omnipresent/order-giving | theology, metaphor, social cognition | evidence of substrate/control architecture independent of religious language | speculative |
| PROPHETIC_INTERFACE | voices/visions as cross-layer information | cognition, sleep, neurological events, culture, fraud | externally verifiable inaccessible information under controlled conditions | speculative |
| INTERVENTION_CONTAMINATION | warning causes predicted act | self-fulfilling prophecy, suggestion, obsession, authority effects | evidence of predictive information unavailable before intervention | speculative |
| WITHDRAWAL_PATCH | ancient active gods -> later hidden God | genre change, institutionalization, secularization, memory, canon formation | independent evidence of intervention-policy change | speculative |
| SIDE_CHANNEL | anomalous perception via hidden physical channel | known auditory/neurological phenomena | reproducible external signal correlated to reports | physically plausible category; specific religious use unestablished |
| MIRACLE_AS_HOST_MUTATION | apparent law violation | legend, misperception, unknown natural event | repeatable state changes violating internal causal model with controlled observation | speculative |
| RESURRECTION_AS_REINSTANTIATION | resurrection mechanical possibility | theology, vision, grief, legend, unknown | evidence of state restoration not reducible to ordinary biology | possible only given simulation premise |
| BIOLOGY_AS_EXPERIMENT | why simulated biological life exists | no simulation; entertainment; research; optimization | host-level evidence of purpose | speculative |
| RECURSIVE_INTELLIGENCE | simulated biology recreates creator-like nonbiological mind | convergent technology without creator relation | host/agent structural correspondence beyond generic computation | speculative |

## Anti-self-sealing rule

"The simulator changed the evidence so we cannot find it" cannot be used as supporting evidence.

"The simulator is powerful enough to explain anything" lowers explanatory value rather than raising it.

## Literary rule

A hypothesis may remain valuable as theology/philosophy even when scientifically untestable.

The book must label which kind of value it has.
