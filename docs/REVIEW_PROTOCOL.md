# Review Protocol

## Gate 1 — Source integrity

Questions:
- Did the cited source actually say this?
- Is the citation to the ancient source or only a modern summary?
- Are work and manuscript witness distinguished?
- Is a quotation translation-dependent?

## Gate 2 — Chronology

- claimed event date;
- composition date;
- witness date;
- later reception date.

No later source silently travels backward in time.

## Gate 3 — Dependence

- Is "multiple attestation" actually one source copied three times?
- Could both sources inherit a shared tradition?
- Is independence merely assumed?

## Gate 4 — Counterevidence

A reviewer must actively search for:
- incompatible source;
- alternative reconstruction;
- chronological impossibility;
- linguistic weakness;
- archaeological contradiction;
- theological redaction.

## Gate 5 — Authorial contamination

Identify every point where the prose knows more than the evidence.

## Gate 6 — Speculative contamination

Simulation/control-system analogies must remain removable without breaking the historical reconstruction.

## Gate 7 — Ethical bias

The manuscript's agency thesis is not allowed to force Yeshua to agree with the author.

## Pass meaning

PASS means "this exact draft respects its declared evidence ceiling."

It does not mean "the scene is what really happened."
