## Literary purpose

The manuscript should stop forcing the arrest crisis through one omniscient narrator.

This lane treats the final night and its aftermath as a small library of incompatible memories.

The reader should experience:
- loyalty and fear at the same time;
- genuine memory and later interpretation at the same time;
- survivor guilt;
- accusation;
- self-justification;
- uncertainty over who knew what;
- the later need to turn a chaotic night into morally legible roles.

## Proposed mini-library

### I. The Witness Who Stayed

Voice:
Mary Magdalene or a Mary-adjacent authored witness.

Function:
Not "Mary tells the true version."
Instead:
Mary remembers what the male disciples did not see because they fled.

Themes:
- bodies;
- fear;
- staying;
- burial;
- the difference between presence and authority.

Guard:
Do not make her a prostitute.
Do not make her secret spouse unless explicitly fictionalized.

### II. Peter's Three Answers

Form:
interior confession / remembered testimony.

Function:
Let denial remain ugly.

Do not excuse it.
Do not convert it into Roman collaboration.

The power of Peter's later authority comes from the unresolved question:
what does a frightened survivor do when the movement begins organizing around him?

### III. Thomas Refuses the Story

Form:
dialogue.

Function:
Thomas resists group certainty.

Not:
"Thomas knew the conspiracy."

Instead:
"Thomas will not say he saw what he did not see."

This lets Johannine doubt become an epistemic literary role without pretending to recover biography.

### IV. Judas: Four Incompatible Accounts

Do not select one hidden Gospel as the answer.

Present four short mutually exclusive possibilities:

A. **The Informer**
He gives location/timing because he has turned against Yeshua.

B. **The Frightened Collaborator**
He thinks arrest means questioning, not execution, and discovers too late what he enabled.

C. **The Loyal Handover**
He believes Yeshua expects or requires the confrontation.

D. **The Scapegoat**
His role is smaller than later memory makes it; the surviving community concentrates the irreversible act into one absent/dead man.

Each must carry an apparatus marker:
`FICTIONAL / LITERARY CONSTRUCTION`.

### V. The Disciple With No Name

Form:
anonymous first-person fragment.

Function:
Represent the crowd of followers whose names disappeared.

He/she fled.

This account should be morally uncomfortable:
the unnamed disciple does not betray Yeshua to an authority; the disciple simply chooses survival.

It prevents the book from pretending betrayal is only what Judas did.

### VI. Memorandum

Form:
Roman/local administrative memorandum.

Function:
Render Yeshua as a public-order problem rather than a theological revelation.

Questions:
- crowd size;
- feast risk;
- arrest timing;
- informant reliability;
- likely charge;
- political utility.

Guard:
This is explicitly reconstructed fiction, not an authentic Roman document.

### VII. The Church Remembers

Form:
later institutional voice.

Function:
Show the pressure to sort:
- martyr;
- apostle;
- witness;
- doubter;
- betrayer.

This voice need not be malicious.

Its danger is that institutions require usable stories.

### VIII. The Heretic Answers

Form:
later counter-gospel voice.

Possible motifs:
- Mary understood.
- Thomas heard the words.
- Judas knew what the Twelve did not.
- Peter survived but survival did not equal comprehension.

This is where Gospel of Mary / Thomas / Judas reception can echo without being back-projected as eyewitness testimony.

### IX. The Historian Interrupts

The narrator returns and refuses to decide what the evidence cannot decide.

Core sentence:

> A tradition can preserve a failure and still change what that failure means.

## Structural effect

This lane directly restores the project's intended New-Testament-like plurality.

Instead of:
`ONE NARRATOR EXPLAINS SIX TRADITIONS`

use:
`SIX VOICES COLLIDE + APPARATUS CONTROLS THE CLAIMS`.

The reader is allowed to notice:
- Peter's failure is survivable;
- Judas's becomes terminal;
- Mary remains but her later authority is contested;
- Thomas can be doubter in one tradition and secret knower in another.

The book does not tell the reader which hidden transcript is "really" true.
