# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation

- freeze project constitution;
- define one-way On-Theo dependency;
- establish evidence and passage provenance contracts;
- build comprehensive source universe;
- build master chronology;
- define narrative canon/order;
- define simulation-layer firewall;
- define authorial thesis without turning it into a required conclusion.

## Phase 1 — Research saturation

Before sustained prose drafting:
- ingest all canonical New Testament source families;
- ingest Hebrew Bible / Septuagint / Second Temple context relevant to Yeshua;
- ingest Dead Sea Scrolls context;
- ingest Josephus, Philo, Greco-Roman and material evidence;
- ingest Christian apocrypha and fragmentary gospels;
- ingest early patristic and hostile witnesses;
- ingest rabbinic countertraditions with dating controls;
- ingest Syriac and Eastern Christian reception;
- ingest Qur'anic and early Islamic Jesus traditions;
- ingest later Jewish mystical/Kabbalistic reception separately;
- register genuinely lost works without fabricating contents.

## Phase 2 — Historical reconstruction

Produce competing models for:
- birth/family traditions;
- John the Baptist relationship;
- kingdom proclamation;
- healings/exorcisms;
- disciples and women followers;
- law, purity and Temple;
- political threat model;
- execution;
- burial;
- resurrection experiences;
- earliest movement.

## Phase 3 — Narrative drafting

Draft book by book only after scene evidence packets exist.

## Phase 4 — Hostile review

For each book:
- source audit;
- chronology audit;
- dependency audit;
- invented-content audit;
- theological-bias audit;
- simulation-backprojection audit.

## Phase 5 — Reader edition

Produce a readable literary edition plus an inspectable critical apparatus. Neither replaces the other.
