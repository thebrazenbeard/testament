## HR-IIV-015 — Synoptic chronology/geography leaks into Book II outline
Severity: HIGH
State: FAIL

Targets:
- manuscript/BOOK_II_YESHUA.md

Problem:
"First proclamation" and especially "The Road South" risk treating a Synoptic-style ministry sequence as recovered chronology. John presents repeated Jerusalem/festival activity and a substantially different narrative framework. Modern historical-Jesus scholarship remains divided over how much Johannine geography preserves independent historical information.

Required repair:
- mark Book II's chapter order as a literary construction rather than recovered itinerary;
- replace "first proclamation" with source-neutral kingdom-proclamation language;
- replace the single "Road South" implication with a final-Jerusalem approach that does not deny possible earlier Judean/Jerusalem activity.
