Purpose: try to make the historical spine fail before manuscript prose can inherit unsupported certainty.

## Review rules

A claim fails this tranche when it:
- counts literary dependence as independent convergence;
- upgrades a source report into an event without marking reconstruction;
- imports a later source into an earlier historical floor;
- bundles propositions with different evidentiary strength;
- uses modern moral categories as though they were ancient source categories;
- lets a chapter title or narrative plan pre-decide an unresolved event;
- turns contextual plausibility into biography.

## Findings

### HR-IIV-001 — Twelve claim outruns its packet support
Severity: HIGH
State: FAIL

Target:
- docs/HISTORICAL_BASELINE.md
- manuscript/BOOK_II_YESHUA.md

Problem:
The baseline labels "a deliberate symbolic circle of twelve" probable, while the repository has no dedicated Twelve historicity packet. Scholarship contains substantial support for a historical Twelve but also a real minority challenge and separate debate over what the number symbolized.

Required repair:
- downgrade the baseline proposition from probable until a dedicated packet is built;
- keep the Twelve in Book II only as a source tradition / research question;
- do not let 1 Cor 15's "the Twelve" prove that Yeshua personally instituted the exact later lists.

### HR-IIV-002 — "Unusual inclusion of marginal people and women" bundles unlike claims
Severity: HIGH
State: FAIL

Target:
- docs/HISTORICAL_BASELINE.md

Problem:
"Marginal people" is a modern analytic category, "unusual inclusion" is comparative/evaluative, and women-followers evidence has a different source basis from tax-collector/sinner meal traditions.

Required repair:
split into source-specific propositions. Do not use "unusual" unless a defined comparator is established.

### HR-IIV-003 — Infancy "communities" over-inferred from texts
Severity: MEDIUM
State: FAIL

Target:
- research/packets/BIRTH_CHRONOLOGY_V1.md

Problem:
Matthew and Luke are extant literary sources. Inferring plural communities preserving traditions may be reasonable but is a social reconstruction not needed for the historical ceiling.

Required repair:
state that Matthew and Luke preserve distinct infancy traditions associated with Bethlehem/Davidic identity; do not make community structure part of the floor.

### HR-IIV-004 — Temple action wording risks false multiple attestation
Severity: HIGH
State: FAIL

Target:
- research/packets/TEMPLE_ACTION_AND_EXECUTION_V1.md

Problem:
"all four canonical Gospels" is a textual observation; "multiply embedded" can sound like multiple independent witnesses. The independence of John from Synoptic Temple-action tradition is unresolved.

Required repair:
separate canonical ubiquity from literary independence. Historical probability may still be argued from explanatory power and scholarship, but not by counting four Gospels as four independent attestations.

### HR-IIV-008 — Baptism outline assumes the visionary event it says is open
Severity: HIGH
State: FAIL

Target:
- manuscript/BOOK_II_YESHUA.md

Problem:
"The Water" says "visionary experience left phenomenologically open," and the next chapter is titled "After the Voice." This preserves uncertainty about mechanism while already assuming that a vision/voice event happened.

## Tranche verdict

FAIL — REPAIRABLE.

The architecture is doing its job: these are mostly boundary leaks, not collapse of the historical model.

The strongest parts surviving this tranche are:
- Roman crucifixion under Pilate;
- early Pauline resurrection proclamation and named leaders;
- broad Jewish/Galilean setting;
- historical association with John;
- source-critical refusal to harmonize Passion chronology;
- separation of empty-tomb questions from resurrection mechanism.

No claim in this review authorizes manuscript prose or merge.
