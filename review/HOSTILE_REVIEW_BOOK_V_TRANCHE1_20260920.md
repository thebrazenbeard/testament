## HR-BV-001 — later-institution motive implication

Original:
> The surviving evidence reveals plurality early, before later institutions tell a cleaner story about unity.

Problem:
"tell a cleaner story" can imply deliberate retrospective concealment or narrative
manipulation as a narrator-level historical conclusion.

Repair:
> The surviving evidence reveals plurality early, before later institutional
> consolidation made some boundaries clearer and some alternatives less visible.

Result:
`REPAIRED`

The new wording describes a historical process without assigning motive.

## HR-BV-002 — Luke/Acts harmonization wording

Original:
> Acts continues the story and often smooths conflict that Paul's own letters leave jagged.

Problem:
"smooths" can read as an asserted authorial intention rather than a comparative
description of narrative presentation.

Repair:
> Acts continues the story and, read beside Paul's own letters, often narrates
> the movement's conflicts in a more harmonized form.

Result:
`REPAIRED`

This preserves the literary comparison while avoiding motive attribution.

## HR-BV-003 — Valentinian field reification

Original:
> A family of serious Christian metaphysical experiments.

Problem:
"family" risks implying a cleaner genealogy/coherence than the current
Ptolemy/Philip/Excerpta/Heracleon/Irenaeus evidence warrants. It also underweights
ritual and interpretive diversity.

Repair:
> A field of related but non-identical Christian interpretive, ritual, and
> metaphysical experiments.

Result:
`REPAIRED`

## HR-BV-004 — direct derivation from one historical center

Original:
> They are many answers generated by one historical gravitational center.

Problem:
"generated by" can imply direct historical descent of each later configuration
from the historical Yeshua rather than from layered memory, textual traditions,
interpretation, community formation, and contest.

Repair:
> They are many answers organized around the remembered, interpreted, and
> contested significance of one historical figure.

Result:
`REPAIRED`

## HR-BV-005 — ritual diversity underrepresented

Problem:
The enriched Book V included *Gospel of Philip* ritual vocabulary but still
risked leaving the impression of one common Valentinian sacramental pattern.

Upstream evidence now shows:
- Ptolemy: external fasting retained within a broader spiritualizing hermeneutic;
- *Gospel of Philip*: baptism/chrism/eucharist/redemption/bridal-chamber complex;
- *Excerpta*: baptism + knowledge, water/Spirit, bread/oil/water, fasting/prayer/
  kneeling;
- Irenaeus: hostile report of materially different redemption practices,
  including some rejection of visible rites.

Repair:
Book V now explicitly says the composite *Excerpta* complicate the picture and
that Irenaeus reports related groups disagreeing even over whether visible rites
were necessary.

Result:
`REPAIRED`

Guard:
The prose still does not assemble these sources into one historical ceremony.
