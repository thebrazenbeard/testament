# Contradiction Register

This file will hold contradictions that the manuscript is not permitted to silently harmonize.

## Seed categories

### Birth
- Matthew/Luke genealogies;
- Herod/Quirinius chronology;
- family residence/geographic sequence.

### Ministry
- Temple action timing;
- number/shape of Jerusalem visits;
- chronology and duration;
- calling/disciple sequences;
- wording and setting of teachings.

### Passion
- Passover chronology;
- trial details;
- cross sayings;
- who visits the tomb;
- tomb state;
- appearance sequence/geography.

### Christology
- adoption/exaltation/pre-existence trajectories;
- relation to Torah;
- relation to Temple;
- resurrection embodiment.

### Later reception
- Christian divine sonship vs Jewish rejection vs Qur'anic correction;
- crucifixion/resurrection interpretations;
- Mary traditions;
- Panthera countertraditions.
