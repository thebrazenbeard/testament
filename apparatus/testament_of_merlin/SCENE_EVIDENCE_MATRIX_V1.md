## Drafting rule

Every scene gets a hidden drafting note naming its highest evidence class. If the prose exceeds that ceiling, the excess is AUTHORED_CONSTRUCTION and must remain traceable here or in a successor apparatus.

## Core paradox

The literary project is strongest where the history is weakest if that weakness is made visible rather than concealed.

The protagonist can therefore be simultaneously:
- intensely human in the narrative;
- historically possible;
- historically unproven;
- and increasingly unreal as later traditions turn him into Merlin.
