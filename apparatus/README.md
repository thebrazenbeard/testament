# Critical Apparatus

The reader-facing manuscript may flow like literature. The apparatus is where the book admits exactly how it was made.

## Passage record

Every consequential passage should eventually have:
- stable passage ID;
- book/chapter/scene;
- manuscript text hash or immutable revision pointer;
- historical propositions asserted;
- upstream On-Theo claim/source/witness dependencies;
- external source citations not yet upstream;
- conflicts and alternatives;
- authored connective tissue;
- invented dialogue/interiority;
- symbolic synthesis;
- simulation-layer use;
- confidence ceiling;
- review status.

## Why this exists

A sentence can be beautiful and still be invented.

A canonical Gospel can preserve ancient tradition and still be theological literature.

A later source can preserve older material and still require an argument.

The apparatus prevents those distinctions from disappearing once prose becomes persuasive.

## Reader editions

Possible outputs:
1. Literary edition — uninterrupted narrative.
2. Annotated edition — compact source/status markers.
3. Critical edition — full passage ledger, variants, dependencies, and notes.

All three derive from the same manuscript; none is allowed to quietly rewrite the evidence state.
