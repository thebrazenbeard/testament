# Scene Packet — The Twelve V1

Status: PRIMARY_BINDING_ACTIVE / NOT_PROSE_READY
Scene ID: SCN-II-TWELVE

## Historical question

Did Yeshua deliberately form a group known as "the Twelve," and what—if anything—can be said about its symbolism and membership?

## Source inventory

### Mark 3:13–19
- formal appointment of twelve;
- named list;
- mission/authority framing.

### Matthew 10:1–4
- probable Markan dependence;
- named list with Matthean ordering/labels.

### Luke 6:12–16
- probable Markan dependence;
- prayer setting;
- named list;
- "apostles" terminology foregrounded.

### Luke 22:28–30 / Matthew 19:28
- Twelve/thrones/tribes restoration imagery in related but non-identical forms.
- source relationship belongs to Synoptic double-tradition debate.

### 1 Corinthians 15:5
Paul's received appearance tradition uses the collective label "the Twelve."

Chronological significance:
- the group-name predates the narrative Gospels in extant evidence.

Limitation:
- "the Twelve" can function as a group designation even when the exact headcount at a moment is not twelve.

## Historical debate

A broad range of Jesus scholarship regards a historical group called the Twelve as likely.

A major interpretive line reads the number symbolically against restoration of Israel/twelve tribes.

Counterposition:
some scholarship argues that the institutionalized Twelve belongs more securely to the post-Easter Jerusalem movement than to Yeshua's own organization.

Controls:
- Scot McKnight, "Jesus and the Twelve," DOI 10.2307/26422271.
- Andries van Aarde, "The historicity of the circle of the Twelve," DOI 10.4102/HTS.V55I4.1634.

## Historical minimum

Strong:
- a group designation "the Twelve" is early, appearing in pre-Gospel Pauline tradition.

Plausible/probable:
- Yeshua had a deliberately selected inner group associated with the number twelve.

Plausible but reconstruction-dependent:
- twelve symbolized Israel/restoration.

Unresolved:
- exact original membership list;
- whether every later named list preserves the historical group unchanged;
- exact distinction among disciples, apostles, and the Twelve;
- exact institutional authority granted by Yeshua.

## Construction budget

Forbidden:
- treating all disciples as members of the Twelve;
- treating every canonical list as independently confirming each name;
- using Acts' Matthias replacement to retroactively solve every first-generation membership question;
- giving each member invented personality/history without separate evidence;
- making restoration symbolism certain rather than argued.

Allowed:
- portray a small inner circle;
- use one declared Gospel list for literary continuity;
- acknowledge that "the Twelve" is both a historical-group question and a symbolic-number question.

## Manuscript strategy

Book II may include a Twelve scene only if the apparatus keeps:
group existence,
exact membership,
and symbolic meaning
as separate propositions.
